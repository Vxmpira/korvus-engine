The scored news feed

Every item carries three numbers: how much it could move index futures, which way it leans, and how sure the scoring is. Low confidence items are labeled as low confidence. Nothing is hidden to make the feed look busier.

The futures board

One board for the main index futures, with last price, change, and volume in one place. Scored news is tied to the contract it touches, so you can see the headline and the tape together.

Cross index SMT divergence

When one index makes a new high or low and a related index does not confirm it, that gap is shown as an SMT divergence. It is a context flag, not a signal. Korvus shows where the divergence sits and leaves the reading to you.

Session context

Each item is placed against the session it lands in. Overnight, pre open, the cash session, and the close each change how a headline reads. The terminal keeps that timing visible.
