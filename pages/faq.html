What is Korvus?

Korvus is a 24/7 market intelligence terminal for index futures traders. It watches the news flow while you are away from the screen, scores what matters, and keeps it in one place so you can do your own research faster.

What does Observer include?

Observer is the free tier. It is the way to see how Korvus works and judge it yourself before you pay anything.

What does Operator include?

Operator is the paid tier. It is 79.99 a year or 9.99 a month. The annual plan saves 39.89 a year compared with paying monthly. Operator also includes the LodeStone TradingView indicator, so the chart tool comes with the terminal.

What is LodeStone?

LodeStone is the chart side of the Korvus stack. It is a session level toolkit built on ICT concepts for index futures, and it does not repaint. On TradingView it is 19.99 a month on its own, or included with Operator.

Does Korvus place trades?

No. Korvus is a research tool. It does not connect to your broker, and it does not enter or exit anything for you. Every decision and every order stays with you.

Is Korvus trading advice?

No. Nothing on Korvus tells you what to buy or sell, and nothing here promises profit. Futures trading carries real risk of loss. Korvus gives you information so you can think; what you do with it is your call.

How are wires scored?

Each news item gets four things: an impact score, a direction, a confidence level, and a written read that explains the reasoning in plain language. The score is a way to sort the feed quickly, not a prediction, and you should check the read against your own plan.

How do I start?

Create a free account and you are in Observer. You can move up to Operator later if the terminal earns it.
