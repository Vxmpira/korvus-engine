SMT divergence, explained for index futures traders.

SMT stands for smart money technique. It compares correlated index futures and asks a simple question: did they both make the new high or low, or did one of them fail? This page explains what that comparison means, how traders study it, and where it breaks down.

What SMT divergence means

Index futures tend to move together. ES and NQ often trend in the same direction. When one of them pushes to a new high and the other does not confirm it, the two charts disagree. That disagreement is the divergence.

The idea is an observation, not a forecast. A divergence tells you the pair is not in agreement at that moment. It does not tell you what happens next.

The pairs traders compare

Most index traders compare contracts that track similar markets. Each pair shows a different angle on the same move.

PairWhat it comparesWhy people watch it
ES and NQBroad market against technology heavy growthThe most common pair. Large cap leadership often shows here first.
ES and YMBroad market against the DowHelps separate a broad move from a narrow one.
NQ and RTYGrowth against small capsShows whether risk appetite is spreading or narrowing.

Questions traders use to study it

These are study prompts, not signals. Work through them on past charts before you think about anything live.

  1. Which pair are you comparing, and are both contracts active in the same session?
  2. Did both markets make the high or low, or only one of them?
  3. Where did the divergence form, near a prior day or week level, or in open space?
  4. What did the broader session do before and after the divergence?
  5. How often did this pattern lead to a clean move in your own sample, and how often did it fail?

Confirmation is not a promise

A divergence can appear and the market can keep going the same way. Many divergences resolve with no clean reversal.

Context changes the read

News, session transitions, and thin liquidity can all produce divergences that mean very little.

Log what you see

Write down each pair, time, and outcome. Your own sample tells you more than any screenshot.

Questions

Is SMT divergence a buy or sell signal?

No. It is an observation that two correlated markets disagree at a point in time. Nothing on this page tells you to buy or sell any instrument.

Which index pair should I start with?

ES and NQ is the most common starting point. Study it on past sessions before you compare other pairs.
