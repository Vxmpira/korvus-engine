Futures Session Times in ET: Globex Hours Guide

Index futures do not stop at the bell. They trade through most of the day and night. This guide shows when each session opens and closes in Eastern Time, so you know which market is awake when you look at a chart.

Eastern Time Globex Asia, London, New York Beginner reference

What Globex means

Globex is the electronic trading platform run by CME Group. Index futures such as the E-mini S&P 500 and E-mini Nasdaq 100 trade on Globex nearly around the clock during the week. The futures market and the cash stock market are not the same thing. Cash stocks open at 9:30 a.m. ET. Futures start earlier and keep going after the cash close.

The week runs from Sunday evening to Friday afternoon, Eastern Time. There is a short daily maintenance break each weekday evening. Exact times can shift with daylight saving changes and exchange holiday calendars, so check the official CME schedule before you rely on any single number.

How to read the handoffs

  1. Note when Asia winds down. Price often reflects what happened there and then keeps moving.
  2. Watch the London open. Many traders treat it as the first big shift of the day.
  3. Expect the New York cash open at 9:30 a.m. ET to bring a new burst of activity and wider moves.
  4. Know the 4:00 p.m. ET cash close. Futures keep trading, but the character of the market often changes.

None of this tells you what price will do. It tells you which room is open. Keep a log of which sessions were active when you looked at a chart. Over time that log is more useful than any single time table.

Common beginner mistakes

Reading a chart in your local time and assuming the session matches. Forgetting daylight saving changes shift the ET clock. Treating the daily maintenance break as a market close. Assuming the cash open and the futures open are the same event.
