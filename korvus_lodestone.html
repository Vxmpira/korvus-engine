Five modules, one registry
Everything you keep five indicators around for,
in one disciplined tool.

Every module is individually toggleable, and every level it draws lives in one shared registry that cleans itself: swept levels fade to quiet stubs or remove themselves, and old levels age out.

01 · Session levels

Session highs and lows

Completed Asia, London, NY AM and NY PM highs and lows, plus prior day and prior week. Each level runs to a fixed cutoff past the live bar with its name at the line, so you always know which pool you are looking at.

02 · Premium / Discount

The dealing range

A range built from confirmed swing points, a completed session, or the prior day, with a faint equilibrium line and subtle zone shading. One glance tells you which half of the range you are paying in.

03 · Resting liquidity

Relative equal highs and lows

Equal swings within a tick tolerance are marked as resting liquidity. Extra equal swings reinforce the existing line instead of stacking duplicates, and the registry cleans them like everything else.

04 · SMT divergence

Cracks between correlated pairs

Your chart's swings compared against a correlated symbol, with automatic NQ and ES pairing and a manual override. Bearish when your chart makes a higher high the pair refuses; bullish on the mirror. Stamped only when the swing confirms.

05 · Volume profile

Restrained by design

No histogram, ever. Profiles are computed internally and only the finished levels print: naked POCs that delete themselves the first time price trades at them, or POC with value area high and low.

+ · The label engine

Labels that cannot overlap

Names sit at a shared right cutoff on subtle backdrop chips. Levels that sit close together merge into one stacked chip whose rows run top to bottom in the same order as the lines beside it, each row carrying its exact price. A combined chip reads as a map of its lines.

The discipline
What LodeStone refuses to do
is the reason to run it.

It never repaints.

Every level, swing, and profile updates on confirmed bars only. Nothing prints and then quietly un-prints after the fact. What you screenshot is what happened.

It never signals.

No arrows, no buy and sell stamps, no scores. LodeStone draws context and leaves the decision where it belongs: with you and your model. If you want a tool that trades for you, this is not it.

It never clutters.

Swept levels fade or remove themselves, only the most recent levels of each type are kept, and the label engine guarantees readable names at any zoom. The chart stays a chart.

Questions
Straight answers.
Do I need a paid TradingView plan?
No. Invite-only script access is granted by the author to your username, and a free TradingView account can run LodeStone once access is granted. A paid TradingView plan changes nothing about how LodeStone works.
I chart on another platform. Is LodeStone useless to me?
LodeStone runs only on TradingView, because it is built in Pine Script. If you execute on another platform, a free TradingView account takes a couple of minutes to create and many futures traders chart there while executing elsewhere. And if you skip the indicator entirely, everything else in your Pro membership works exactly the same.
Does it repaint?
No. Swings, levels, and profiles are used only after bar confirmation, so nothing is drawn and then removed when the bar closes differently. This costs a few bars of patience and buys you a chart you can trust.
Will it tell me when to buy or sell?
No, deliberately. LodeStone is context and decision support: it shows you where the levels, liquidity, and divergences are. Your entry model, your risk, and your decisions stay yours. We do not sell signals and we do not promise profits.
What happens if I cancel Pro?
LodeStone access is part of the membership, so it is revoked when the membership ends. Your TradingView account, charts, and layouts are untouched; the indicator simply stops being available to your username. Rejoin Pro and access is granted again.
Can I see or copy the source code?
No. Invite-only scripts are closed source on TradingView: the platform runs the script and you see the drawings, never the code. That protection is exactly why we distribute it this way.
How fast is access granted?
Grants are done manually by a human, normally within 24 hours of saving your TradingView username on your account page. You will find LodeStone under Indicators, in the Invite-only scripts tab.
