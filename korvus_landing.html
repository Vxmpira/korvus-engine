What you get
A research desk for independent futures traders.

Most independent traders have charts and an execution platform, but no research desk. The context behind a move arrives late, scattered across feeds, group chats, and open tabs. Korvus consolidates that work: it gathers the news, evaluates it, and presents the result in a form you can read in seconds.

01 / COVERAGE

Continuous news coverage

The engine monitors financial newswires and market commentary 24 hours a day, across a 59-instrument universe: index futures, the megacap equities that drive them, currencies, rates, energy, metals, and agriculture. A story that breaks overnight is already collected, summarized, and scored before you sit down. Nothing depends on you watching the wires yourself.

02 / ANALYSIS

Every story scored and explained

Each item is summarized in plain language and rated on three axes: impact (HIGH, MED, or LOW), direction (bullish, bearish, or neutral), and confidence. The instruments a story touches are tagged on it, and routine noise is filtered out so the feed stays readable. One pass tells you which of the day's headlines deserve attention and why.

03 / STRUCTURE

Cross-index divergence, measured

Through US market hours, Korvus compares NQ, ES, and YM against their session highs and lows and reports whether the three indexes are confirming each other or diverging. Many index traders treat divergence at an extreme as an early warning of a turn. Korvus measures the condition from market data instead of leaving you to eyeball three charts.

04 / ONE SCREEN

Full session context in one place

The terminal places the scored feed beside a macro backdrop covering the dollar, rates, gold, oil, credit, and volatility, the Asia, London, and New York session map, a funds board of index heavyweights, and live market television from third party broadcasters. Everything you would normally check before and during a session, on a single screen.

Before you decide
What Korvus is, and what it is not.

Trading tools tend to oversell. Before you create an account, here is a plain statement of what you are buying and where the limits sit.

What it is

An information terminal, described plainly.

Korvus collects financial news, evaluates each story with an automated analysis engine, and presents the result next to live market context. Its job is to shorten the time between something happening in the market and you understanding what it means for the indexes.

It does not place trades, generate entry signals, or predict outcomes, and it will not promise you profitability. Trading index futures carries substantial risk, and every decision remains yours. Treat the scoring as a well organized starting point for your own judgment, because that is what it is.

We built it this way on purpose. A tool that is honest about its limits is a tool you can actually rely on.
