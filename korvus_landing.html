    What Korvus is, and what it is not.

    Korvus collects financial news and filings, evaluates each one with an automated analysis engine, and presents the result next to market context. Its job is to shorten the time between something happening and you understanding what it means for the indexes.

    It does not place trades, generate entry signals, or predict outcomes, and it will not promise you profitability. Compass scores are research summaries of public data, not recommendations. Trading index futures carries substantial risk, and every decision remains yours.

    We built it this way on purpose. A tool that is honest about its limits is a tool you can rely on.
